PREVIEW / EXPERIMENTAL

Not implemented yet. This page is a placeholder. No Proof of Play data is collected, stored or shown here.

Proof of Play is intended to show evidence of what a screen actually played. That evidence must come from the player's own playback path (for example a capture of the playback output or the player's playback log).

The audience USB camera is never used as playback evidence. It looks at the audience, not at the screen content, and Visual Memory frames from it must not be presented as proof that any content played.
