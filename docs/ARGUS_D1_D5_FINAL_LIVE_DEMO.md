## 2. Limits to state out loud

- Producer-to-receipt latency is **not measured**. `time_uncertainty_ms` (about 252 ms in the sessions) is the configured 250 ms bound plus measured jitter, not a validated absolute value.
- The 30 s slot shows the first 30 s of Ad A (121.7 s video). Ad B is 15.0 s, so it loops twice; its 15 s evidence point lies on the loop boundary. Matching uses the best reference over all offsets, so this does not distort the Ad B result; the "reference offset" column for Ad A frames can differ by about 1 s from the capture offset.
- One gRPC frame per capture point, three per ad; the optional 1 FPS evidence experiment was **not run**.
- The preview is the Weston/VOP2 composite of presentation video and graphics. It is not proof that a person looked at the screen. HTML, HLS and DRM paths are untested.
- D4 searches manually indexed regions only. Image similarity does not identify a person or object with certainty.
- The Visual Memory store has 1 757 older frames without an embedding (embedding backlog; `backfill_on_startup` is off). The dashboard shows this as a status note. It is not new.

## 5. Walk-through (about 18 minutes)

Open `http://192.168.0.165:20300/`.

### A. Audience Measurement (2 min)
Click **Audience**. Show the live camera preview with person boxes, gaze/attention, counters and graphs. Say: "YOLOX and RetinaFace run on the NPU at about 29 FPS, and nothing else on this page is simulated." Point at the FPS value (about 29).

### B. Visual Memory (3 min)
Click **Visual Memory**. Show: frames stored, with image, embeddings, sampling interval 5 s, queue health "ok", drops 0. Open the newest gallery card: source, timestamp, image path/size, SHA-256. Compare its time with "newest frame" in the tiles; they agree. Say: "Every five seconds a frame becomes a JPEG on the SD card with a row in SQLite, then the NPU creates a MobileNetV2 embedding." Camera frames may show people: crop or hide this view if the audience is external.

### C. Full-frame Visual Search (2 min)
Open a stored frame and click **Find Similar Frames** (or upload a prepared people-free JPEG). Show the top five, similarity scores, timestamps and sources; open one result. Say: "This is visual similarity, not identity."

### D. Object Search (3 min)
Open a stored frame that shows a cup or box. Draw a box, save the region, repeat on one or two other frames, then search with an indexed region. Show top matches, scores and the box overlay. Say: "Search runs over regions someone indexed. It is not automatic discovery of every object."

### E. Live Weston/VOP2 preview (1 min)
Click **Proof of Play**, scroll to **Display output**. Show the live HDMI picture, observed FPS (about 4.6 to 5), frame age, provenance "Weston/VOP2 writeback", black/frozen indicators. The camera view in Audience keeps running at about 29 FPS in parallel.

### F. Proof of Play (8 min)
On `/#pop` show the **Advertisement registry** (Ad A: 121.7 s, Ad B: 15.0 s, 30 s slot; SHA-256; 122 + 122 and 15 + 15 reference frames, both profiles). Explain: "Reference frames were cut from the original MP4s, not from captured evidence."

1. **Verify Correct Ad.** The page shows the session, "Waiting for the player", then elapsed time against 30 s and frames 1/3, 2/3, 3/3 at about 3, 15, 27 s. After about 32 s: **Playback corroborated**. Open the session: each captured frame beside its reference, similarity, uncertainty about 250 ms, provenance `weston_vop2_writeback`, and "Why this result".
2. **Verify Wrong Ad.** Ad B plays while Ad A was expected. Result **Mismatch**: low similarity to Ad A (about 0.58), and the closest registered media is Ad B (about 0.90).
3. **Verify Interrupted Ad.** Confirm the dialog. Ad A is stopped at about 10 s. Result **Incomplete**: the early frame matches but only 10 of 30 s played.
4. **History.** Show the rows, filters, backend column, evidence counts, **Export CSV** and **Export JSON**. Open any row for the per-frame evidence.
5. Optional: the genuine "Capture unsupported" session from today (reuse from history; trigger it with `break_capture` only from the command line).

Each scenario takes about 32 s (interrupted about 15 s). Run the three in the order above; wait for the verdict before starting the next, since sessions run back to back on the player.

Fallback comparison: set the backend selector to `brightscript_screenshot` (same buttons) to compare the old method.

## 6. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Action |
|---|---|
| Buttons greyed out | `allow_test_jobs` is false, or a session is active. Check `/api/pop/status`. |
| "Waiting for the player to start" never ends | The autorun is in loop mode. `/storage/sd/pop/enabled` is missing or empty; make it non-empty and reboot (DWS `PUT /api/v1/control/reboot`). `adapter.boots` must be at least 1. |
| First frame "capture unsupported" or STALE | The producer wakes on the first request (the 503 / STALE at start is expected; the service retries). Persistent failure: check Display output panel, then `/api/display/status`. |
| Mismatch expected but result is Incomplete / inconclusive | Do not force it. Report the scores; they are real. |
| Camera FPS low | Low light lowers USB camera FPS (about 19 to 20 FPS); compare at equal light. |
| Display output says "frozen for N s" | In the revised autorun (below) this should only appear if the presentation did not resume. In the first demo autorun the ad was stopped on its last frame, which the OS health check correctly reported as static (frame difference 0.0000). If it appears now: check `idle_started` events in the session timeline and the autorun version (`autorun.brs` SHA-256 in section 8). |
| Dashboard unreachable | Wait about 90 s after any reboot; check `/api/pop/status`. |

Never open a root console while presenting: it stops the BrightScript presentation and the DWS. Do not enable Telnet. The gRPC socket is local (Unix) only.

## 8. Revision: the presentation resumes after every test (installed and validated on the XT5, 2026-11-06)

Why: the first demo autorun stopped the video at the end of a slot (static last frame, flagged "frozen") and, in job mode, showed nothing before the first test. The original autorun (SHA-256 `83076b0b…609ab`) is a single loop: `roVideoPlayer` full-screen rectangle 1920x1080, `SetLoopMode(true)`, `PlayFile({Filename:"meet-brightsign.mp4"})`, then it only prints video events. There is no other playback logic, so the idle state reuses exactly that.

Autorun state machine (`scripts/pop/pop_autorun.brs`, job mode): `IDLE` (looping presentation) -> `PREPARING` (idle video stopped, event port drained and replaced) -> `PLAYING` (the job's media; events carry the job's session id) -> `COMPLETED` (`session_end` is written first, so verification starts at once) -> `RESUMING` (video stopped, port drained and replaced, presentation restarted) -> `IDLE`. Slot end, interruption, a failed `PlayFile` and a start timeout all go through RESUMING; a capture failure only fails evidence, never the playback loop, so the presentation returns in that case too. The next job is read only after RESUMING has finished.

Event isolation: the idle video writes no event with a session id (only the session-less `boot` and `idle_started` lines), so it cannot create or change a verification session; leftovers of the stopped idle video are drained and the port is replaced before a job's `PlayFile`; an "ended" event before `play_started` is ignored; every test event carries the job's own session id. The verification engine, thresholds and the three-frame offsets are unchanged.

Geometry note: the idle player sets the 1920x1080 rectangle, as the original does, and the job media plays in that same rectangle, which is the geometry the display-output references were validated against. The first hardware runs played jobs without a rectangle (default full screen); similarities may therefore change slightly (expected: up). Report the new numbers; do not adjust thresholds.

Dashboard: a state strip at the top of `/#pop` shows Ready (normal presentation playing) / Starting advertisement / PLAYING <ad> with the seconds left of the slot and the evidence count / Verifying / Verification completed (resuming). It is derived from the real sessions; nothing is set by the page. The preview keeps updating, and the completed result stays in Latest session and Playback History.

Host validation: `tests/browser/pop_demo_cdp.py` 43/43 (real vm_api_server, bridge, dashboard server, OS-service double, headless Chrome; the player emulator writes the same `boot`/`idle_started`/session events); the BrightScript parses without syntax errors (brighterscript; only the BrightSign-specific components are unknown to it). The BrightScript itself cannot run on the host: the state machine is exercised for the first time on the XT5.
